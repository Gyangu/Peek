# Competitor and reference icon matrix

Surveyed 2026-08-03. What is recorded here is the icon language visible on
current public product pages, App Store listings and brand assets. It exists to
locate the crowded regions and the room left between them; it copies no artwork.

## What the clustering says

1. **The crowded region for database products**: cylinders, animals and
   mascots, product initials, a white mark on blue.
2. **The crowded region for developer tools**: angle brackets and folded
   ribbons, nested squares, neon gradients on black, stacked windows.
3. **The crowded region for "viewing" products**: eyes, magnifiers, lenses,
   circular visualisations.
4. **The gap peek can occupy**: make "two sources merging into one visible
   state" into a structural mark; depict neither a database nor an organ of
   observation.
5. On colour, start from the UI's `#171A21`, `#4D9CFF` and `#4EC9A0`, but judge
   direction in black and white, so that colour cannot mask a problem with the
   silhouette.
